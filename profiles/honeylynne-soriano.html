ABOUT THE FACILITATOR

Ms. Honeylynne A Soriano is a dedicated ICT Educator and Company System Developer with more than ten years of professional experience in website development, business systems implementation, workflow automation, and project management for international companies. She is currently a Teacher II under the Department of Education, teaching Grade 10 Computer Programming.

She teaches Grade 10 TLE – Computer Programming with emphasis on HTML, CSS, website development, and coding fundamentals. She develops Daily Lesson Plans (DLPs), instructional materials, laboratory activities, and competency-based assessments. She implements project-based learning through Project WEB (Website Education Builder) and facilitates practical coding sessions in the ICT Laboratory.

She integrates real-world web development practices into classroom instruction and prepares learning resources aligned with the MATATAG Curriculum.

Passionate about bridging industry practices with classroom instruction to prepare students for careers in Information Technology.
