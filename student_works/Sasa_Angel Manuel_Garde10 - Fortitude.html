WHAT IS NUTRITION?

Nutrition is the study of how food and drink nourish the body, affecting growth, development, and overall health. It involves the processes
by which the body takes in, absorbs, and uses chemical compounds—called nutrients—to produce energy, build tissue, and sustain vital functions

DO to protect the environment
while getting nutrients

To protect the environment while staying healthy, eat more plant-based foods, buy local produce,
and stop throwing away food. These steps lower pollution, save water, and make sure your body gets the vitamins it needs.

advantages in our environment of eating vegetables

  • Lower Greenhouse Gases
  • Water Conservation
  • Land Protection
  • Cleaner Soil
