"Healthy Diet, Healthy Life"

Celebrate Nutrition Month!

Nutrition Month is a time to recognize the importance of healthy eating and maintaining a balanced lifestyle. It reminds us that choosing nutritious foods, staying physically active, and practicing healthy habits can improve our overall well-being and help prevent illnesses. By eating a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and protein-rich foods, we can give our bodies the nutrients needed to grow, learn, and stay strong. Let us celebrate Nutrition Month by making healthier choices every day and encouraging our families and communities to do the same, because good nutrition is the foundation of a healthier and brighter future.

Fruits

Rich in vitamins, minerals, and fiber that keep you healthy.

Vegetables

Provide essential nutrients that help protect your body.

Milk

Excellent source of calcium for strong bones and teeth.

Fish

Contains protein and healthy fats that support heart health.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables every day.
  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water.
  • Eat balanced meals.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Limit sugary drinks and junk food.
