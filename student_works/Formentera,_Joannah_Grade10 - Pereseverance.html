Let me ask you, Do you know what "Protecting Nutrition and the Environment Together is about?.
Healthy people and a healthy planet go hand in hand. The food we eat depends on clean air, fresh water, fertile soil, and a balanced environment. By making healthy food choices and taking care of nature, we can build a brighter future for ourselves and the next generation. Protecting nutrition and the environment is not just a responsibility—it is a way of life

What healthy foods come from nature?


Healthy foods that come from nature include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, nuts, seeds, beans, fish, eggs, and fresh milk. These foods provide essential vitamins, minerals, protein, and fiber that help keep our bodies strong and healthy. 🌱🍎🥕🥜🐟

Good nutrition helps our bodies grow strong, stay healthy, and fight diseases. At the same time, protecting the environment ensures that we continue to have fresh fruits, vegetables, grains, and clean water. Pollution, deforestation, and food waste can Harm both our health and the Earth's natural resources.

Everyone can make a difference through simple actions. Eating nutritious meals, reducing food waste, recycling, planting trees, and conserving water all contribute to a healthier environment. When we care for nature, we also protect the food systems that nourish our communities.

Working together as families, schools, and communities can inspire positive change. By choosing sustainable habits today, we create a healthier, greener, and more sustainable future for everyone.

(Ways to Help)

  • Eat balanced and nutritious meals every day.
  • Reduce food waste by taking only what you can eat.
  • Bring reusable bags, bottles, and containers.
  • Plant trees and care for gardens
    • .
  • Recycle and segregate waste properly.
  • Save water and electricity.
  • Keep your surroundings clean.

(Steps to Protect Nutrition and Nature)

  1. Choose healthy and locally grown foods.
  2. Avoid wasting food during meals.
  3. Use reusable items instead of single-use plastics.
  4. Join tree-planting and community clean-up activities.
  5. Practice recycling and composting at home.
  6. Encourage family and friends to live sustainably.
  7. Continue learning about nutrition and environmental protection.
