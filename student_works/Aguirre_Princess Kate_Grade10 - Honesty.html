Essential Nutrients & Food Groups

A balanced diet requires a blend of macro and micronutrients. Here is a breakdown of everyday foods to power your body:

Fresh Produce

Rich in vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber essential for digestion and immunity.

Whole Grains

Provides complex carbohydrates for sustained energy throughout the day.

Lean Proteins

Crucial for muscle repair, growth, and overall bodily functions.

Recommended Daily Plate Ratio

Follow this general breakdown to structure your daily meals effectively:

Plate Category Recommended Proportion Primary Benefits Example Foods
Vegetables & Fruits 50% (Half Plate) High in Fiber, Antioxidants, & Micronutrients Spinach, Squash, Apples, Berries
Whole Grains 25% (Quarter Plate) Sustained Energy & Blood Sugar Control Brown Rice, Oats, Quinoa, Whole Wheat
Protein Sources 25% (Quarter Plate) Muscle Repair & Immune Support Fish, Poultry, Tofu, Legumes
