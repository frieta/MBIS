HEALTHY PLATE, HEALTHY BODY

Health and a good diet are essential for living a strong, active, and balanced life. Eating a variety of nutritious foods, such as fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats, provides the body with the vitamins and minerals it needs to function properly. Drinking enough water and limiting sugary drinks, processed foods, and excessive amounts of salt and fat can also help maintain good health. Along with regular physical activity, a healthy diet supports growth, strengthens the immune system, improves energy levels, and reduces the risk of many diseases. By making healthy food choices every day, people can improve both their physical and mental well-being and enjoy a better quality of life.
